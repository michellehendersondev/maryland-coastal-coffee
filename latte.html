A Latte Enjoy Ocean City

Address: 109 N Atlantic Ave, Ocean City, MD 21842

Website:
www.alatteenjoy.com

A Latte Enjoy is a coffee shop on the boardwalk in Ocean City. Offerings include locally roasted coffee, espresso drinks, smoothies and teas. They offer indoor and outdoor seating as well as takeout. The shop features "quality ingredients and delicious creations" made with locally sourced organic freshly ground coffee beans.

Region Waterfront View Walkable to Waterfront
Eastern Yes Yes

3 Things to Try:

  1. Nutella Frappe
  2. Blueberry banana smoothie
  3. Dragon Bowl
